Brazil is a country of extraordinary beauty and diversity. Home to the Amazon Rainforest — the world's largest tropical rainforest — as well as iconic landmarks like Christ the Redeemer in Rio de Janeiro and the stunning Iguazu Falls. Brazil is also known for its vibrant culture, samba music, and world-famous Carnival celebration.
