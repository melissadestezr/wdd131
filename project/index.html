Why Cats?

A few compelling reasons to love them even more

🧠

Highly Intelligent

Cats have around 300 million neurons in their cerebral cortex — far more than dogs. They solve problems, remember routines, and adapt to new environments quickly.

❤️

Great Companions

Studies show that owning a cat reduces stress and lowers the risk of heart disease. Their purring vibrates at a frequency known to promote healing.

🌙

Low Maintenance

Cats are independent by nature. They self-groom, use litter boxes instinctively, and are perfectly content entertaining themselves — ideal for busy lifestyles.

🎭

Unique Personalities

Every cat has a distinct personality shaped by breed, upbringing, and environment. From playful and affectionate to aloof and mysterious — there is a cat for everyone.

Feline Curiosities

Fascinating things you might not know about cats

01

Cats sleep 12–16 hours a day

As natural predators, cats conserve energy for hunting. Even domestic cats maintain this instinct, napping throughout the day in short cycles.

02

A group of cats is called a clowder

While cats are famously independent, feral cats often form colonies with complex social structures, hierarchies, and cooperative cub-rearing behaviors.

03

Cats can make over 100 vocal sounds

Dogs can make about 10 sounds. Cats, however, have developed a rich vocal repertoire specifically to communicate with humans — including the manipulative "solicitation purr."

04

Cats always land on their feet

This is mostly true. Cats have a flexible backbone and no functional clavicle, allowing them to perform a "righting reflex" mid-fall to orient themselves feet-first.
