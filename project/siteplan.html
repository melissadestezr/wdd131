01

Site Name

Cats World

Cats World was chosen because it is short, memorable, and inclusive, it suggests a complete universe dedicated to cats rather than a single narrow topic. The name works well for both: casual visitors who love cats and people actively looking for breed or care information.

Optional domain availability: cats-world.info or catsworld.pet

02

Site Purpose

Cats World is a three-page informational website that celebrates cat culture and helps visitors learn about different breeds and how to care for their pets. The site provides:

Home Page

Cat curiosities and dynamically generated fun facts powered by JavaScript. The page serves as an engaging entry point with rotating facts and visual appeal to encourage exploration.

Breeds Catalog Page

A filterable catalog of cat breeds displaying key characteristics such as size, temperament, coat type, and origin. A JavaScript-powered filter system and a favorites list saved to localStorage allow users to bookmark and revisit breeds they love.

Contact Page

A form that lets visitors submit a photo of their own cat or send questions. This builds community and encourages user participation.

03

Scenarios

These are real questions a target visitor, a cat lover or a prospective cat owner might have when arriving at the site:

  • Which cat breed is best for a small apartment and someone who works long hours?
  • What is a fun or surprising fact about cats that I can share with my friends?
  • How do I know if a Persian cat is the right breed for my lifestyle?
  • Can I submit a photo of my cat to be featured on the website?
  • What are the grooming needs of a Maine Coon compared to a Siamese?
04

Color Schema

The palette draws from warm earthy tones, burnt sienna, deep brown, and cream, evoking a cozy natural feeling that complements cat photography. A soft sage green adds a calm accent.

Primary Headings, buttons, links, highlights #D97B4A
Secondary Navigation bar, footer, strong text #5C3D2E
Background Page background, card base #FDF6EE
Accent Badges, borders, subtle highlights #A8C5A0
Body Text Paragraphs, labels, icons #2E1F16
